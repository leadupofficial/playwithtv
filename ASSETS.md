# Third-party assets

Everything not listed here (art, sounds, levels, text) is original and made in this repository. Each pack keeps
its licence file next to the files. Games also list what they use in their manifest's `licenceInventory`
(`packages/protocol/src/catalog.ts`).

| Asset | Used by | Licence | Source | In this repo |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Kenney Space Kit (rocket parts, meteors, rocks, craters, hangar, dish, platform, astronaut, generator) | Squat Rocket | CC0 1.0 | https://kenney.nl/assets/space-kit | `apps/web/public/assets/kenney-space-kit/` |
| Kenney Sci-Fi Sounds (engine, explosion, force field, laser, low rumble) | Squat Rocket | CC0 1.0 | https://kenney.nl/assets/sci-fi-sounds | `apps/web/public/assets/kenney-sci-fi-sounds/` |
| Kenney Interface Sounds (pluck, confirmations, maximise, tick, error) | Squat Rocket | CC0 1.0 | https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds | `apps/web/public/assets/kenney-interface-sounds/` |
| MediaPipe Tasks Vision 0.10.35 wasm runtime and Pose Landmarker (lite) model | camera mode (phone) | Apache-2.0 | https://ai.google.dev/edge/mediapipe, npm `@mediapipe/tasks-vision@0.10.35` | `apps/web/public/vendor/mediapipe-0.10.35/` (checksums in `SOURCE.txt`) |

Kenney assets are CC0 (no attribution required); we credit Kenney (www.kenney.nl) here anyway.
