kitplat artwork (Monsoon Courier, Lantern Lane, Stepwell Secrets)

Every image and sprite sheet in this folder is original artwork made for Play With TV. The models, materials,
lighting and scenery were built from primitives in Blender (Python scripts in tools/blender/kitplat/) and rendered
by those scripts; no third-party models, textures, HDRIs, fonts or images were used, and nothing was copied or
adapted from any other game. Rebuild everything with:  sh tools/blender/kitplat/render_all.sh

The sounds these games play are the CC0 1.0 Kenney packs already shipped in ../kenney-audio/ and ../kenney-jingles/
(their licence files are next to them), plus tones synthesised in code.
