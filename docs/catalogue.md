## Built games: motion mappings

### 🌀 Gravity Maze 3D

Hold your phone flat like a tray and tilt it to roll the ball past holes and moving walls into the goal. Take turns: fastest total time wins.

Hold: **flat** · gyroscope: unused · assets: all visuals and sounds (drawn and synthesised in code) (original); three.js renderer (MIT)

- **roll**: tilt the maze left/right
- **pitch**: tilt the maze forward/back

### 🚀 Sky Gyro Racer

Fly through floating gates in space. Turn your phone like a steering wheel to steer, tip it to climb or dive, push it towards the TV to boost.

Hold: **upright** · gyroscope: optional · assets: all visuals and sounds (drawn and synthesised in code) (original); three.js renderer (MIT)

- **roll**: steer left/right
- **pitch**: climb / dive
- **push**: boost (uses energy)

### 🌈 Tilt Jumper Worlds

Bounce up an endless sky tower through four worlds. Turn your phone like a wheel (or use ◀ ▶) to steer, stomp critters, grab gems and fire a rocket or shield. Up to 4 climb side by side: highest climb wins.

Hold: **upright** · gyroscope: unused · assets: all visuals and sounds (drawn and synthesised in code) (original)

- **roll**: steer the bouncing hero left/right
- **flick-up**: fire the stored power-up (rocket or shield)

### ⚔️ Motion Blade Trial

Glowing plums, lemons, gourds and lanterns fly up: slice them with your blade, chain combos, catch the golden lotus and dodge spiky mines. Drag on the touchpad, flick with motion or slash with buttons. Up to 4 blades on one screen.

Hold: **upright** · gyroscope: unused · assets: all visuals and sounds (drawn and synthesised in code) (original)

- **roll**: aim the blade left/right
- **pitch**: aim the blade up/down
- **flick-left**: slash left
- **flick-right**: slash right
- **flick-up**: slash up
- **flick-down**: slash down

### 🎳 Bowling Master

Ten-pin bowling for up to four: turn your phone to aim, push it towards the TV to roll (harder = faster), twist for hook. Or hold ROLL on the buttons for a power meter, or swipe up on the touchpad. Real strike and spare scoring.

Hold: **upright** · gyroscope: optional · assets: all visuals and sounds (drawn and synthesised in code) (original)

- **roll**: swing the aim arrow
- **flick-left**: step left
- **flick-right**: step right
- **twist-left**: hook left
- **twist-right**: hook right
- **push**: roll the ball (a strong push: power roll)

### 🏒 Air Hockey Motion

Glide your striker and smash the puck into the goal: drag on the touchpad, steer with the joystick, or tilt your phone. Play a friend or the CPU. First to 7 wins.

Hold: **any** · gyroscope: unused · assets: all visuals and sounds (drawn and synthesised in code) (original)

- **roll**: move the striker left/right on your half
- **pitch**: move the striker up/down
- **push**: smash (a quick lunge at the puck)

### 🧠 Tamil Trivia Royale

Quiz rounds in Tamil and English. Tap your answer's colour on your phone, or turn the phone to aim and push to lock in.

Hold: **upright** · gyroscope: unused · assets: all visuals and sounds (drawn and synthesised in code) (original); questions (original)

- **roll**: move your marker across the four answers
- **push**: lock in the answer

### 🥊 Ring Kings Boxing

Box a CPU champion or a friend: punch, block and dodge on your phone's buttons, or box with motion (push to jab, flick to hook). Land the SUPER punch, beat the 10-count!

Hold: **upright** · gyroscope: unused · assets: all visuals and sounds (drawn and synthesised in code) (original)

- **push**: jab (a strong push: power punch)
- **flick-left**: hook
- **flick-right**: hook
- **flick-up**: uppercut
- **pitch**: tip forward to step in, slightly back to step out, far back to guard
- **roll**: lean to slip a punch
- **shake**: get up after a knockdown
